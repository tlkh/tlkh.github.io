An influence beyond Apple Silicon

From independent Go, Swift, and Rust implementations to a commercial Mac interface and Linux/NVIDIA monitoring, asitop has influenced tools beyond its original Python implementation.

  1. mactop ↗

    Go monitor that credits asitop as its original inspiration.

  2. NeoAsitop ↗

    Independent Swift implementation that describes itself as asitop-inspired.

  3. pumas ↗

    A Rust reimplementation of asitop.

  4. FluidTop ↗

    An enhanced fork for newer Apple Silicon, terminal compatibility, and local-AI workloads.

  5. Mac Power Monitor ↗

    A commercial Mac app whose Apple Silicon summary window is designed to resemble asitop's display.

  6. asitop-csv-logger ↗

    A fork adding CSV logging for historical analysis.

  7. actop ↗

    Credits asitop as inspiration while using an independent architecture and codebase.

  8. DGXTOP ↗

    Extends the inspiration to Ubuntu and NVIDIA DGX Spark hardware monitoring.

  9. SiliconScope ↗

    Formerly WhisPlayInfo; names asitop and NeoAsitop among the motivations for its native SwiftUI dashboard.

  10. basitop ↗

    Credits asitop as a reference for powermetrics plist parsing.

  11. silicon-monitor / simonlib ↗

    Its Apple monitoring module documents asitop as its basis; the credit applies to that component.

  12. macmon ↗

    Acknowledges asitop as the original tool in its related-project list, establishing a predecessor relationship.

First-author research · ACL 2022 25 citations

Towards Better Characterization of Paraphrases

As first author, I introduced Word Position Deviation (WPD) and Lexical Deviation (LD) to measure changes in word order and vocabulary between paraphrases without expert annotation. The paper uses these metrics to evaluate datasets, improve model generalisation, and select paraphrases for data augmentation and robustness testing. Using MRPC and PAWS as case studies, I showed how the metrics reveal structural and lexical differences that conventional overlap scores can miss, while guiding dataset improvements and more controllable paraphrase generation.

I released paraphrase-metrics, an open-source Python library with almost 5k downloads. Hanyang University independently used WPD and LD to evaluate paraphrasing quality in its FCMR benchmark (published at ACL 2025), demonstrating external research adoption and validation of the metrics.
